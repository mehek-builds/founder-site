# About page AI-feel audit

Date: 2026-07-22
URL: https://mehekmandal.com/about
Scope: rendered desktop, tablet, and mobile About page

## Verdict

The page does not look AI-generated overall. Its editorial typography, asymmetrical portrait composition, personal admissions, and physical bookshelf interaction feel intentional and authored.

- Visual AI feel: 2/10
- Copy AI feel: 4/10
- Overall AI feel: 3/10
- Strongest concern: My three rules

## Section review

### Hero

Copy: "Hi. I promise I do other things."

Verdict: Human. The line is self-aware, slightly awkward in a good way, and specific to the page's purpose. It avoids generic personal-brand language.

### Intro paragraph

Copy: "At heart, I'm a builder. I like meeting people all over the world, hearing what gets in their way, and building the simplest thing that fixes it. That is the whole job."

Verdict: Medium AI feel.

The phrases "At heart, I'm a builder," "hearing what gets in their way," and "the simplest thing that fixes it" form a polished founder-copy sequence. Each idea is valid, but the paragraph is broad enough to fit many founders. "That is the whole job" adds personality, but still reads like a crafted positioning line.

### What the bet costs

Verdict: Strongly human.

The unfinished degree, wound-down startup, unanswered email, and unresolved final sentence are specific and unflattering. AI-generated personal branding usually resolves tension into a lesson. This section leaves the tension open.

### 57 countries

Copy: "I travel to meet people. Most of what I have built started as someone else's complaint."

Verdict: Low AI feel.

"57 countries" is concrete. The paragraph beneath it is highly compressed and aphoristic. "Someone else's complaint" is memorable, but it sounds like a line optimized for quotation rather than a lived detail.

### Off the clock

Verdict: Strongly human.

The 22 trails, six marathons, Dubai, January, and inability to run in June create a timeline that belongs to one person. The mild self-mockery also feels natural.

### My three rules

Verdict: High AI feel.

Rule 1, "Always try. There is no failing, only learning," is generic motivational language. Rule 3, "Open heart, open mind," is even more interchangeable. Both could appear on a coaching site, an AI-generated founder page, or a poster.

Rule 2 starts generically with "Explore, and talk to everyone," but recovers with "Connections cost less than the plane ticket." The second half sounds like Mehek.

The numbered set of three also resembles a common generated personal-brand pattern: three universally agreeable values with no evidence attached.

### Calls to action

Verdict: Neutral.

"See the work" and "Get in touch" are standard interface labels. They do not add personality, but they do not create an AI impression either.

### Bookshelf

Verdict: Visually human, editorially mixed.

The physical shelf interaction and custom spines feel handcrafted. The selection leans heavily toward famous founder, finance, and self-improvement books, which risks reading like a default founder reading list. The component would feel more personal with brief reasons for each book or one or two surprising titles outside the standard canon.

## What makes the page feel authored

- The page admits uncertainty instead of converting every setback into a lesson.
- The portrait is presented as an editorial object rather than a generic profile card.
- The copy contains real numbers, dates, places, and unfinished personal threads.
- The layout avoids standard AI patterns such as card grids, icon circles, purple gradients, and centered sections.
- The bookshelf is an interaction with a clear physical metaphor.

## Quick wins

1. Replace rules 1 and 3 with behaviors or opinions that only Mehek would plausibly write.
2. Remove "At heart, I'm a builder" and start the intro with a concrete action, person, or recurring situation.
3. Add one specific example beneath "57 countries" instead of ending on a polished aphorism alone.
4. Add short personal annotations to books, especially why each changed a decision or belief.

- Design score: A-
- AI slop score: A-
- Copy specificity: B
- Personal credibility: A
